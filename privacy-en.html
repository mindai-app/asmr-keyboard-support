Privacy Policy

公開準備中。本番購入・本番広告は停止中です。
Preparing for release. Production checkout and ads are disabled.

What you type

We do not collect, save or transmit typed text, composition text or typing history. Text needed for typing and suggestions is processed temporarily in memory and is not sent to our servers or ad providers. Japanese conversion and English suggestions run on your device. We do not build a personal learning history. Storage or transmission by the app you type into is governed by that app and its privacy policy.

Settings and purchases

Theme, volume, sound and haptic preferences and verified purchase status are stored on your device for the companion app and keyboard. Purchases are designed to use Apple on iOS and Google Play on Android. We do not receive card details. Production checkout is currently disabled. Purchase checks and restoration communicate with the store. If verification fails, the keyboard falls back to free Basic PC; this does not cancel a subscription. No app account is required. The Android purchase verification server is not connected yet. Before connecting it, we will disclose how purchase tokens and related data are handled.

Ads

Ads are designed only for optional companion-app previews. Typed text is never passed to ad providers. The iOS keyboard extension contains no ad SDK. On Android the companion app and keyboard share one application, but the ad SDK starts from the companion preview button and is not called by keyboard processing. The Android release candidate includes Google Mobile Ads and UMP, with automatic ad startup and advertising-ID permissions disabled. Production ads are disabled on both platforms. Even test ads may let Google and participating advertising providers process IP addresses, device/app-related identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Test ads still use network connections. Disabling advertising IDs and personalization does not eliminate all data processing. We will review actual processing and store disclosures before enabling production ads. When an adult chooses an ad, Google UMP displays required consent choices. Users may decline or manage choices; the companion app provides advertising privacy options where required.

Children and students

Typing and free Basic PC need no purchase or ad. Before a preview, we optionally ask for age. The numeric age is not saved or sent; only under 18, adult, or unanswered is stored locally in the companion app. This group is not sent to the keyboard or an ad provider. Preview actions for under-18 and unanswered users do not initialize an ad SDK or request an ad; a 60-second preview is available without ads. Adults may preview after voluntarily watching an ad. Both previews stay in the companion app; a verified Premium subscription is needed to use paid themes as the system keyboard. Previews do not charge money. Purchases follow store confirmation and family approval settings. The calculation gate is not legal parental consent.

Permissions

We do not request microphone, contacts or device location permissions. iOS Full Access is used for local key sounds. Enabling it also permits networking at the OS level, but this keyboard has no code to send typed text externally. Typing works with Full Access off. The keyboard reads companion-app settings and verified purchase status; typed text is not shared. Android internet permission is application-wide for companion ads and purchase checks, but typing and conversion do not initiate networking. Ad providers may infer an approximate region from an IP address.

Contact

Contact 99.rockyou@gmail.com. Email addresses and messages you voluntarily send for support are used to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send private typed text or screenshots containing personal information. You may request deletion of support correspondence at this address.
