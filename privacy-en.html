Privacy Policy

公開準備中。本番購入・本番広告は停止中です。
Preparing for release. Production checkout and ads are disabled.

What you type

We do not collect, save or transmit typed text, composition text or typing history. Text needed for typing and suggestions is processed temporarily in memory and is not sent to our servers or ad providers. Japanese conversion and English suggestions run on your device. We do not build a personal learning history.

Settings and purchases

Theme, volume, sound and haptic preferences and verified purchase status are stored on your device for the companion app and keyboard. Purchases are designed to use Apple on iOS and Google Play on Android. We do not receive card details. Production checkout is currently disabled. Purchase checks and restoration communicate with the store. If verification fails, the keyboard falls back to free Basic PC; this does not cancel a subscription. No app account is required. The Android purchase verification server is not connected yet. Before connecting it, we will disclose how purchase tokens and related data are handled.

Ads

Ads are designed only for optional companion-app previews. Typed text is never passed to ad providers. The iOS keyboard extension contains no ad SDK. In Android test builds the companion app and keyboard share one application, but the ad SDK starts from the companion preview button and is not called by keyboard processing. The current Android release build contains no ad SDK. Production ads are disabled on both platforms. Even test ads may let Google process IP addresses, device/app-related identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Test ads still use network connections. Disabling advertising IDs and personalization does not eliminate all data processing. We will review actual processing and store disclosures before enabling production ads.

Children and students

Typing and free Basic PC require no purchase or ad viewing. An ad reward allows only a 60-second preview inside the companion app and cannot unlock the installed keyboard. Watching an ad does not make a purchase. Purchases use store confirmation and family approval controls where configured. An in-app confirmation screen is not age verification or legal parental consent. Production ads remain disabled until requirements for the audience, including children, have been verified.

Permissions

We do not request microphone, contacts or location access. iOS Full Access is used for local key sounds and shared settings, not to send typed text. Typing works without Full Access. On Android, enable the keyboard as a system input method. Android test builds with ads have application-wide internet permission, but keyboard text processing stays on the device.

Contact

Contact 99.rockyou@gmail.com. Email addresses and messages you voluntarily send for support are used to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send private typed text or screenshots containing personal information. You may request deletion of support correspondence at this address.

Updates
